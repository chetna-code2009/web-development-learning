COMMUNICATION

Communication is the process of exchanging information,ideas,thoughts,feelings and messages between a sender and a receiver through a suitable medium.

IMPORTANCE OF COMMUNICATION

1. Exchange of Information-it helps people share ideas,facts and instructions.

2. Coordination-it helps diffrent people and department coordinate their activities.

3. Decision making-mangers need accurate information to make decisions.

4. Avoids misunderstanding-clear communication reduces errors and confusion.

5. Increases producivity-when employees clearly understand their responsibilities, work becomes more efficient.
