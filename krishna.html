🌸HARE KRISHNA MAHAMANTRA🌸

The Hare Krishna Mahamantra is a sacred sanskrit mantra associated with devotion to Lord Krishna and the practice of bhakti-yoga.It is traditionally chanted as a form of meditation, prayer, and spiritual remembrance.

The Mahamantra


Hare krishna, Hare Krishna
Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare
Hare Rama,Hare Rama,
Rama Rama, Hare Hare
The mantra consists of the name Hare, Krishna, and Rama

.In the Vaushnava tradition, chanting these names is understood as a way of remembering the Supreme Lord and developing devotion, peace, and spritual awareness.ISKCON describes the mahamantra as the "great mantra for delibrance."

Why is it chanted

Chanting the Hare Krishna Mahamantra is a central practice of bhakti yoga.It can be performed individually, known as japa,or collectively through devotional singing called kirtan. For many devotees, the repeated chanting of the mantra is a way to calm the mind, express devotion, and remember Krishna throughout daily life.
