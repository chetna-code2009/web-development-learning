Lord Krishna

About Lord Krishna

Lord Krishna is one of the most revered deities in Hinduism. He is known for his wisdom, compassion, courage and devotion. Krishna played an important role in the Mahabharata and guided Arjuna through the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita.

Qualities of Lord Krishna

  • Wisdom
  • Compassion
  • Courage
  • Leadership
  • Devotion
  • Love and Kindness

Important Places in Krishna's Life

Place Importance
Mathura Birthplace of Lord Krishna
Gokul Krishna spent his childhood here
Vrindavan Known for Krishna's childhood and divine activities
Dwarka Krishna established his kingdom here

Krishna and the Flute

The flute is one of the most famous symbols associated with Lord Krishna. It represents peace, love and divine music.
